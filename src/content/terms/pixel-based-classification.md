---
term: "Pixel-based classification"
category: earth-observation
aliases: []
short: "Pixel-based classification assigns each pixel to a class using only its own spectral values, without regard to its neighbours."
related: ["object-based-image-analysis", "supervised-classification"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It is simple and works well for moderate-resolution imagery. At high resolution it can produce a noisy, salt-and-pepper result.
