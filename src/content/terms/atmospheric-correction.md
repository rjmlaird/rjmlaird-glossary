---
term: "Atmospheric correction"
category: earth-observation
aliases: []
short: "Atmospheric correction removes the effects of scattering and absorption by gases and aerosols from satellite imagery, to estimate surface reflectance."
related: ["radiometric-calibration", "change-detection"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Without it, the same surface looks different on different days and at different heights. It is essential for comparing images over time and for calculating indices reliably.
