---
term: "Doppler shift"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "The Doppler shift is the change in the observed frequency or wavelength of a wave caused by relative motion between source and observer."
related: ["spectral-line", "transit-method"]
updated: 2026-10-17
---

Light from something moving away appears redder, and from something moving towards us appears bluer. By measuring how far [spectral lines](/spectral-line/) move, astronomers can work out speeds, which helps in studying stars and finding planets.
