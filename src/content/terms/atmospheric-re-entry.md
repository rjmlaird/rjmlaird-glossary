---
term: "Atmospheric re-entry"
category: space
aliases: ["re-entry"]
short: "Atmospheric re-entry is the return of an object from space into the dense atmosphere, where compression heating usually breaks it up."
related: ["deorbit", "demisability", "design-for-demise"]
updated: 2026-10-24

A controlled re-entry targets a safe area. Uncontrolled re-entry is predicted only roughly, and some fragments of larger objects can reach the ground.
