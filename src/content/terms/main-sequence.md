short: "The main sequence is the long phase of a star's life in which it fuses hydrogen into helium in its core, and the band that such stars occupy on the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram."

The Sun is a main-sequence star. A star's mass sets its position on the sequence and how long it stays there, with massive stars burning out much faster.
