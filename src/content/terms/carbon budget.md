# Carbon budget

**Definition:** A carbon budget is the total amount of carbon dioxide that can still be emitted while keeping global warming below a chosen limit, with a stated level of likelihood.

**In plain language:** Think of it as a remaining allowance. Estimates depend on the temperature target and on scientific uncertainties, so the numbers differ between sources and should always be cited with their assumptions.
