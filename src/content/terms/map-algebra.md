---
term: "Map algebra"
category: earth-observation
aliases: []
short: "Map algebra applies mathematical operations to raster layers cell by cell, for example adding layers or applying conditions."
related: ["raster-data", "ndvi"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Operations can be local (a single cell), focal (a neighbourhood), zonal or global. Indices such as NDVI are calculated this way.
