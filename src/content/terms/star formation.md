# Star formation

**Definition:** Star formation is the process by which dense regions within cold clouds of gas and dust collapse under gravity to form stars.

**In plain language:** Clumps in a cloud pull themselves together, heat up and eventually ignite nuclear fusion. Leftover material can form discs in which planets and smaller bodies such as comets take shape.
