---
term: link budget
category: space
aliases:

short: The accounting of gains and losses between transmitter and receiver.

A link budget adds up every gain and loss between a transmitter and a receiver: antenna gains, cable losses, distance and atmospheric absorption. If what arrives is above the receiver's sensitivity by a safe margin, the link works.

**Why it matters:** it tells you whether a satellite can actually reach your ground station before anyone builds anything.
