short: "Direct air capture uses chemical processes to pull carbon dioxide out of ambient air, so that it can be stored or used."

Because carbon dioxide is dilute in air, about 0.04%, it needs a lot of energy and is costly today. Counted as a removal only if the carbon dioxide is stored durably.
