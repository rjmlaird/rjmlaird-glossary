---
term: "Greenhouse effect"
category: sustainability
aliases: []
short: "The greenhouse effect is the warming of a planet's surface caused by atmospheric gases that absorb outgoing thermal radiation and re-emit part of it back towards the surface."
related: ["carbon-budget"]
updated: 2026-10-17
---

Sunlight warms the Earth, which radiates heat back out. Gases such as water vapour, carbon dioxide and methane slow that heat's escape, keeping the planet warmer than it would otherwise be. Adding more of these gases strengthens the effect.
