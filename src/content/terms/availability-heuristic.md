---
term: "Availability heuristic"
category: science-communication
aliases: []
short: "The availability heuristic is judging how likely or common something is by how easily examples come to mind."
related: ["confirmation-bias", "media-framing"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It was described by Amos Tversky and Daniel Kahneman. Vivid or recent events, such as dramatic news stories, seem more frequent than they are.
