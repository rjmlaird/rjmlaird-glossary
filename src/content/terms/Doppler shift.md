# Doppler shift

**Definition:** The Doppler shift is the change in the observed frequency or wavelength of a wave caused by relative motion between source and observer.

**In plain language:** Light from something moving away appears redder, and from something moving towards us appears bluer. By measuring how far [[spectral line|spectral lines]] move, astronomers can work out speeds, which helps in studying stars and finding planets.
