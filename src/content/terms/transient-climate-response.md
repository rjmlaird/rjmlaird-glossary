---
term: "Transient climate response (TCR)"
category: climate-science
aliases: ["TCR"]
short: "Transient climate response is the global warming at the moment carbon dioxide has doubled, when concentrations rise by 1% a year, before the climate has fully adjusted."
related: ["climate-sensitivity", "equilibrium-climate-sensitivity"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It is lower than equilibrium climate sensitivity because the oceans are still absorbing heat. The IPCC's Sixth Assessment Report gave a best estimate of 1.8 °C, with a likely range of 1.4 to 2.2 °C.
