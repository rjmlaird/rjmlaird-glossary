---
term: "Synthetic aperture radar (SAR)"
category: earth-observation
aliases: ["SAR"]
short: "Synthetic aperture radar is an active imaging radar that uses the motion of a satellite or aircraft to synthesise a long antenna and produce fine-resolution images."
related: ["active-remote-sensing", "remote-sensing"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It works day and night and sees through cloud, which makes it valuable for floods, ice and ground movement. Images look different from optical ones because they record radar backscatter and are affected by speckle.
