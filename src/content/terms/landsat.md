short: "Landsat is a series of US land-imaging satellites, run by NASA and the US Geological Survey, that has observed the Earth's land surface continuously since 1972."
related: ["sentinel-2", "change-detection"]
updated: 2026-10-24

Its multi-decade archive, at 30-metre resolution in the main multispectral bands, is the longest of its kind and is free to use. It is central to studies of long-term land change.
