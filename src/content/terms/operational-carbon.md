---
term: "Operational carbon"
category: sustainability
aliases: []
short: "Operational carbon is the greenhouse gas emissions from the energy used to run a building or product while it is in use."
related: ["embodied-carbon", "life-cycle-assessment"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It contrasts with embodied carbon, which is released making and delivering it. As grids decarbonise, embodied carbon becomes a larger share of the lifetime total.
