---
term: "Data quality"
category: web-architecture
aliases: []
short: "Data quality is how fit data is for its intended use, judged by dimensions such as accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, validity and uniqueness."
related: ["data-validation", "data-lineage"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Quality is relative to purpose, so data that is fine for one use may be inadequate for another. Measuring and monitoring it is more reliable than assuming it.
