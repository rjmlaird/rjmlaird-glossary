---
term: "Global warming potential (GWP)"
category: climate-science
aliases: ["GWP", "GWP100"]
short: "Global warming potential compares how much heat a greenhouse gas traps with carbon dioxide, over a stated time horizon, usually 100 years."
related: ["co2-equivalence", "methane-slip"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

By definition carbon dioxide has a GWP of 1. The IPCC's Sixth Assessment Report gives methane a 100-year GWP of about 27 to 30, and a much higher value over 20 years because methane is short-lived.
