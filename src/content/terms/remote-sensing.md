---
term: "Remote sensing"
category: earth-observation
aliases: []
short: "Remote sensing is acquiring information about an object or area without direct contact, typically by measuring radiation with instruments on satellites, aircraft or drones."
related: ["earth-observation", "spectral-line"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Instead of touching or sampling something, you read it from a distance using light or other waves. The raw measurement is then converted into useful quantities such as temperature, vegetation cover or gas levels.
