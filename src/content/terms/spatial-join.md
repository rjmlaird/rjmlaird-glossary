---
term: "Spatial join"
category: earth-observation
aliases: []
short: "A spatial join combines the attributes of two layers according to how their features relate in space, for example which polygon each point falls within."
related: ["overlay-analysis", "buffer-analysis"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Relationships include intersects, within, contains and nearest. It is the geographic equivalent of a table join.
