---
term: "Orthorectification"
category: earth-observation
aliases: []
short: "Orthorectification corrects an image for distortions caused by terrain relief and sensor tilt, so that its scale is uniform like a map."
related: ["georeferencing", "digital-elevation-model"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Without it, a hill or tall building appears displaced from its true position. It needs an elevation model and information on the sensor's position and orientation.
