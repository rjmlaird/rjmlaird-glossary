---
term: "Accretion disc"
category: space
aliases: ["accretion disk"]
short: "An accretion disc is a rotating disc of gas that spirals in towards a central object such as a black hole, a neutron star or a young star."
related: ["black-hole", "active-galactic-nucleus", "protoplanetary-disc"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Friction and compression heat the gas, which radiates strongly, often at X-ray wavelengths for compact objects.
