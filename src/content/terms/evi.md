term: "Enhanced Vegetation Index (EVI)"

aliases: ["EVI"]
short: "The Enhanced Vegetation Index is a vegetation index that adds a blue band to correct for atmospheric effects and canopy background, and saturates less than NDVI in dense vegetation."
related: ["ndvi", "atmospheric-correction"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It is widely used with MODIS data for tropical forests and other high-biomass areas, where NDVI levels off.
