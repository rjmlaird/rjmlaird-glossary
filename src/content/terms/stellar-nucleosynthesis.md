---
term: "Stellar nucleosynthesis"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "Stellar nucleosynthesis is the creation of chemical elements by nuclear fusion inside stars and in stellar explosions."
related: ["main-sequence", "supernova"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Stars fuse hydrogen to helium and, in massive stars, heavier elements up to iron. Most heavier elements form in processes such as neutron capture in supernovae and neutron-star mergers.
