---
term: "Medium Earth orbit (MEO)"
category: space
aliases: ["MEO"]
short: "Medium Earth orbit is the region between low Earth orbit and geostationary orbit, roughly 2,000 to 35,786 kilometres altitude."
related: ["low-earth-orbit", "geostationary-orbit"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Navigation constellations such as GPS and Galileo fly here, at about 20,000 to 23,000 kilometres. The radiation environment is harsher than in LEO.
