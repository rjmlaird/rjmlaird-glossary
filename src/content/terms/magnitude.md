# Magnitude

**Definition:** Magnitude is a logarithmic scale for the brightness of astronomical objects. A difference of 5 magnitudes corresponds to a brightness ratio of 100.

**In plain language:** The scale runs backwards: smaller numbers mean brighter objects, and very bright objects can have negative values. Apparent magnitude describes how bright something looks from Earth, while absolute magnitude compares objects at a standard distance.
