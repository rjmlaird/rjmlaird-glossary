short: "Albedo is the fraction of incoming light that a surface or body reflects, from 0 (absorbs everything) to 1 (reflects everything)."

updated: 2026-10-17
---

Bright surfaces such as fresh snow reflect much of the sunlight that hits them, while dark surfaces such as open water absorb more. Albedo affects how much energy a planet takes in, so it matters for both climate and for how bright planets and asteroids appear.
