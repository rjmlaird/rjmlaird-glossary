---
term: "Sun-synchronous orbit (SSO)"
category: space
aliases: ["SSO"]
short: "A sun-synchronous orbit is a near-polar orbit whose plane turns by about one degree per day, so a satellite passes over any latitude at the same local solar time."
related: ["low-earth-orbit", "earth-observation"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It gives consistent illumination, which is why many Earth observation satellites use it. Typical altitudes are 600 to 800 kilometres at an inclination near 98 degrees.
