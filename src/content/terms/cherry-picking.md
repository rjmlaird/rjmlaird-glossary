---
term: "Cherry-picking"
category: science-communication
aliases: ["cherry picking"]
short: "Cherry-picking is selecting only the data or examples that support a claim while ignoring evidence against it."
related: ["confirmation-bias", "false-balance"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It can be done with real figures, such as a short time window that hides a long-term trend. Looking at the full dataset and its context guards against it.
