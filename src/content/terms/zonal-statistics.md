---
term: "Zonal statistics"
category: earth-observation
aliases: []
short: "Zonal statistics summarise the values of a raster, such as the mean, sum or maximum, within each zone defined by a set of polygons."
related: ["raster-data", "vector-data"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

An example is average elevation or total rainfall for each catchment or district. It links raster data to vector areas.
