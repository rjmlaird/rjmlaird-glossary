---
term: "Watershed delineation"
category: earth-observation
aliases: ["drainage basin delineation"]
short: "Watershed delineation traces the boundary of the area that drains to a chosen point, normally using flow directions derived from an elevation model."
related: ["catchment-analysis", "digital-terrain-model"]
updated: 2026-10-24

Errors in the elevation model, such as pits and flat areas, must be corrected first. It underpins flood, water-quality and hydrology studies.
