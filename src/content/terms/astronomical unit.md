# Astronomical Unit

**Definition:** The astronomical unit (AU) is a unit of length, defined as exactly 149,597,870,700 metres. It is close to the average distance between the Earth and the Sun.

**In plain language:** A convenient yardstick for distances within the Solar System. Earth is about 1 AU from the Sun, and other planets are described as multiples of that.
