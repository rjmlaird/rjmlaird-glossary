term: "Representative Concentration Pathways (RCPs)"
category: climate-science
aliases: ["RCP", "RCPs"]
short: "Representative Concentration Pathways are four greenhouse gas concentration trajectories, named by their 2100 radiative forcing, used in the IPCC's Fifth Assessment Report."
related: ["shared-socioeconomic-pathways", "radiative-forcing"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

The four pathways are RCP2.6, RCP4.5, RCP6.0 and RCP8.5, in watts per square metre. They have largely been superseded in new modelling by scenarios that combine pathways with Shared Socioeconomic Pathways.
