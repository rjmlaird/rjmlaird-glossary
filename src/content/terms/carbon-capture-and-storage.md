---
term: "Carbon capture and storage (CCS)"
category: sustainability
aliases: ["CCS", "carbon capture, utilisation and storage"]
short: "Carbon capture and storage captures carbon dioxide from a power plant or industrial process, transports it and stores it permanently underground."
related: ["direct-air-capture", "negative-emissions"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It reduces emissions from sources that are hard to decarbonise, such as cement. It does not remove carbon dioxide already in the air, unlike direct air capture.
