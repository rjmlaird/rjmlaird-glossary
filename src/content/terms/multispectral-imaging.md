---
term: "Multispectral imaging"
category: earth-observation
aliases: ["multispectral"]
short: "Multispectral imaging records an image in several separate wavelength bands, typically three to around a dozen, such as blue, green, red and near-infrared."
related: ["hyperspectral-imaging", "spectral-signature"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Different surfaces reflect each band differently, so band combinations can separate vegetation, water and bare ground. Sentinel-2 and Landsat are multispectral.
