short: "In astronomy, metallicity is the proportion of a star's mass made of elements heavier than helium, which astronomers loosely call metals."
related: ["stellar-nucleosynthesis", "spectroscopy"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It is usually expressed relative to the Sun, as [Fe/H]. Metal-rich stars are more likely to host giant planets, and older stars are generally more metal-poor.
