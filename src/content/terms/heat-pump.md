---
term: "Heat pump"
category: sustainability
aliases: ["heat pumps"]
short: "A heat pump moves heat from a cooler source such as outside air, the ground or water into a building, using electricity to drive a refrigeration cycle."
related: ["electrification", "district-heating"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Because it moves heat rather than generating it, a heat pump typically delivers about three or more units of heat per unit of electricity. Its efficiency falls as the temperature gap widens.
