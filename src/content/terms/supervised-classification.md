category: earth-observation
aliases: []
short: "Supervised classification trains an algorithm on labelled examples, then uses it to assign every pixel or object to a class."
related: ["unsupervised-classification", "ground-truthing"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

The labels usually come from field data or interpreted imagery. Accuracy depends on the quality and coverage of the training samples, so a separate set is kept to test it.
