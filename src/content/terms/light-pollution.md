---
term: "Light pollution"
category: sustainability
aliases: []
short: "Light pollution is excessive or misdirected artificial light that brightens the night sky and affects people, wildlife and observation."
related: ["magnitude"]
updated: 2026-10-17
---

Street lighting, signs and buildings scatter light into the sky, hiding faint stars and making astronomy harder. Shielded, well-aimed and appropriately bright lighting reduces the problem and often saves energy.
