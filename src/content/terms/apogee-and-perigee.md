---
term: "Apogee and perigee"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "For an object orbiting the Earth, perigee is the point of closest approach and apogee is the point farthest away."
related: ["geostationary-orbit", "astronomical-unit"]
updated: 2026-10-17
---

Orbits are rarely perfect circles. An elliptical orbit swings closer to Earth at perigee, where the object moves fastest, and farther out at apogee, where it moves slowest. Other bodies have their own terms, such as perihelion and aphelion for the Sun.
