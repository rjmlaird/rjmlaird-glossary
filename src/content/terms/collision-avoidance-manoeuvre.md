---
term: "Collision avoidance manoeuvre"
category: space
aliases: ["collision avoidance manoeuvre (CAM)", "avoidance manoeuvre"]
short: "A collision avoidance manoeuvre is a planned thruster burn that changes a spacecraft's orbit to increase its miss distance from another object."
related: ["conjunction-assessment", "collision-probability"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Operators decide on one after a conjunction assessment shows a high collision probability. Each manoeuvre uses fuel and can interrupt normal operations.
