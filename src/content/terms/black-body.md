---
term: "Black body"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "A black body is an idealised object that absorbs all radiation falling on it and emits radiation whose spectrum depends only on its temperature."
related: ["emissivity", "spectral-line", "greenhouse-effect"]
updated: 2026-10-17
---

No real object is a perfect black body, but stars and planets can often be approximated as one. This lets us estimate temperatures from the colour and intensity of the light they give off.
