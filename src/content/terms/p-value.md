---
term: "P-value"
category: science-communication
aliases: ["p value"]
short: "A p-value is the probability of seeing results at least as extreme as those observed, if the null hypothesis and the model were true."
related: ["statistical-significance", "confidence-interval"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It is not the probability that the null hypothesis is true, nor the probability that the result is due to chance. It is often misinterpreted.
