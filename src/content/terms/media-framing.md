---
term: "Media framing"
category: science-communication
aliases: ["framing"]
short: "Media framing is how the selection, emphasis and wording of coverage shape how people understand an issue."
related: ["false-balance", "availability-heuristic"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

The same facts can be framed as a risk, an opportunity or a conflict, which leads to different reactions. Awareness of framing helps both journalists and audiences.
