short: "Uncertainty quantification is the process of estimating how uncertain a result is, and expressing it, for example as ranges or probabilities."
related: ["confidence-interval", "ensemble-modelling"]

It separates what is known from what is not. Communicating uncertainty well avoids both false precision and the impression that uncertain means unknown.
