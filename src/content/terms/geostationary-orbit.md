---
term: "Geostationary orbit"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "A geostationary orbit is a circular orbit about 35,786 km above the equator, in which a satellite takes one sidereal day to circle the Earth."
related: ["apogee-and-perigee"]
updated: 2026-10-17
---

Because it keeps pace with Earth's rotation, the satellite appears to stay fixed in the sky. This is useful for communications and weather satellites that need to watch the same region continuously.
