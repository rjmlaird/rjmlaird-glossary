# Remote sensing

**Definition:** Remote sensing is acquiring information about an object or area without direct contact, typically by measuring radiation with instruments on satellites, aircraft or drones.

**In plain language:** Instead of touching or sampling something, you read it from a distance using light or other waves. The raw measurement is then converted into useful quantities such as temperature, vegetation cover or gas levels.
