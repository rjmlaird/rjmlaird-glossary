---
term: "Net-zero emissions"
category: climate-science
aliases: ["net zero", "net-zero"]
short: "Net-zero emissions means that human-caused greenhouse gas emissions are balanced by human-caused removals over a given period."
related: ["greenhouse-gas-removal", "carbon-budget", "greenhouse-gas-inventory"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

The definition depends on scope: which gases, which emissions and what counts as a removal. Credible plans cut emissions first and use removals only for what is hard to abate.
