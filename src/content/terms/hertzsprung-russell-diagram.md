---
term: "Hertzsprung–Russell diagram"
category: space
aliases: ["HR diagram", "H-R diagram"]
short: "The Hertzsprung–Russell diagram plots stars by luminosity against surface temperature, with temperature conventionally increasing to the left."
related: ["main-sequence", "red-giant", "white-dwarf"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Stars fall into groups, including the main sequence, giants and white dwarfs. It is a key tool for reading the evolutionary stage of a star.
