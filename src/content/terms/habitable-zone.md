---

aliases: ["circumstellar habitable zone"]
short: "The habitable zone is the range of distances from a star where a rocky planet could have liquid water on its surface, given a suitable atmosphere."
related: ["transit-method", "radial-velocity-method"]
updated: 2026-10-24

Being in the zone does not make a planet habitable. Atmosphere, magnetic field and the star's activity also matter.
