---
term: "Low Earth orbit (LEO)"
category: space

short: "Low Earth orbit is the region of orbits from roughly 160 to 2,000 kilometres above the Earth's surface."
related: ["medium-earth-orbit", "geostationary-orbit", "orbital-debris"]

Most crewed spaceflight, Earth observation satellites and the newer broadband mega-constellations operate here. It is also where most tracked debris is found.
