---
term: "Epistemic vigilance"
category: science-communication
aliases: []
short: "Epistemic vigilance is the set of cognitive mechanisms we use to assess whether communicated information, and the person giving it, can be believed."
related: ["epistemic-trust", "confirmation-bias"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It was proposed by Dan Sperber and colleagues in 2010. It explains why trust in a source, and coherence with what we already know, strongly affect belief.
