# Transit method

**Definition:** The transit method detects a planet around another star by measuring the small, periodic dip in the star's brightness when the planet passes in front of it.

**In plain language:** If a planet crosses our line of sight to its star, it blocks a tiny fraction of the light. The size and timing of the dip indicate the planet's size and orbital period.
