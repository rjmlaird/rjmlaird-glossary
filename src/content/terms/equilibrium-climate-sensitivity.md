---
term: "Equilibrium climate sensitivity (ECS)"
category: climate-science
aliases: ["ECS"]
short: "Equilibrium climate sensitivity is the long-term global warming once the climate has fully adjusted to a doubling of atmospheric carbon dioxide."
related: ["climate-sensitivity", "transient-climate-response"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

The IPCC's Sixth Assessment Report gave a best estimate of 3 °C, with a likely range of 2.5 to 4 °C. Full adjustment takes centuries because the deep ocean warms slowly.
