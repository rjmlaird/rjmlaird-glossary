---
term: "Anthropogenic climate change"
category: climate-science
aliases: ["human-caused climate change"]
short: "Anthropogenic climate change is the long-term warming and wider climate shift caused by human activity, mainly burning fossil fuels, land-use change and agriculture."
related: ["greenhouse-effect", "radiative-forcing"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Greenhouse gases from these activities have raised atmospheric concentrations well above pre-industrial levels. It is distinguished from natural variability such as volcanic eruptions, solar changes and orbital cycles.
