---
term: "Demand response"
category: sustainability
aliases: []
short: "Demand response is changing when electricity is used, in response to price signals or grid conditions, to help balance supply and demand."
related: ["grid-flexibility", "smart-grid"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Examples include pre-heating buildings, delaying electric vehicle charging and shifting industrial processes. It is a cheaper alternative to building extra generation or storage for short peaks.
