term: "Bioenergy with carbon capture and storage (BECCS)"
category: sustainability
aliases: ["BECCS"]
short: "BECCS grows biomass, uses it for energy, and captures and stores the carbon dioxide released, so that the process can remove carbon on balance."
related: ["greenhouse-gas-removal", "carbon-capture-and-storage"]

---

It could deliver negative emissions, but at large scale it needs a lot of land and water. Its net benefit depends on how the biomass is grown and harvested.
