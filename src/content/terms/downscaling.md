---
term: "Downscaling"
category: climate-science
aliases: []
short: "Downscaling derives finer-resolution local climate information from coarse global climate model output."
related: ["bias-correction", "ensemble-modelling"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Dynamical downscaling runs a regional model nested in the global one. Statistical downscaling relates large-scale patterns to local observations. Both inherit the biases of the model they start from.
