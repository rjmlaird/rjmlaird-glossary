---
term: "Gravitational waves"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "Gravitational waves are ripples in spacetime caused by accelerating masses, such as merging black holes or neutron stars."
related: ["black-hole", "neutron-star"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

They were first detected directly by LIGO in September 2015, from two merging black holes. They carry information that light does not.
