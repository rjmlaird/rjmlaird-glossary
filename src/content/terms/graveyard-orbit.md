---
term: "Graveyard orbit"
category: space
aliases: ["disposal orbit"]
short: "A graveyard orbit is a disposal orbit, a few hundred kilometres above geostationary orbit, to which retired geostationary satellites are moved."
related: ["geostationary-orbit", "post-mission-disposal"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Raising the satellite clears the busy geostationary ring for working spacecraft. It needs some fuel to be reserved for the move.
