---
term: "Energy return on investment (EROI)"
category: sustainability
aliases: ["EROI", "EROEI"]
short: "Energy return on investment is the ratio of usable energy delivered by a source to the energy invested in obtaining it."
related: ["life-cycle-assessment", "levelised-cost-of-energy"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

A high ratio means a source gives back much more than it costs to build and operate. Results depend heavily on where the system boundary is drawn.
