---
term: "Hyperspectral imaging"
category: earth-observation
aliases: ["hyperspectral"]
short: "Hyperspectral imaging records hundreds of narrow, contiguous wavelength bands for every pixel, giving a nearly continuous spectrum."
related: ["multispectral-imaging", "spectral-signature"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

The detail allows materials, crop stress or minerals to be identified from their spectral signature. The cost is much larger data volumes and harder calibration.
