---
term: "Statistical significance"
category: science-communication
aliases: []
short: "A result is statistically significant when it would be unlikely to arise by chance alone if there were no real effect, by a pre-set threshold, commonly p below 0.05."
related: ["p-value", "effect-size"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Significance says nothing about size or importance. With large samples even trivial effects can be significant.
