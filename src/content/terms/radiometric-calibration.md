---
term: "Radiometric calibration"
category: earth-observation
aliases: []
short: "Radiometric calibration converts the raw digital numbers recorded by a sensor into physical units such as radiance or reflectance."
related: ["atmospheric-correction", "remote-sensing"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It uses pre-launch measurements and in-flight references such as the Sun, the Moon or stable ground sites. It must be done before values from different sensors or dates are compared.
