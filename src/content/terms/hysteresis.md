---
term: "Hysteresis"
category: climate-science
aliases: []
short: "Hysteresis is when a system's state depends on its history, so reversing the driver does not return it along the same path."
related: ["tipping-point", "climate-feedback"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

In climate terms, an ice sheet that melts at a given temperature may not regrow when temperatures fall back to that level. Hysteresis is why tipping points can be so hard to undo.
