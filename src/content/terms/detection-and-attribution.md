---
term: "Detection and attribution"
category: climate-science
aliases: ["attribution science", "extreme event attribution"]
short: "Detection and attribution are statistical methods that first show a climate change is larger than natural variability, then assess how much each cause contributed."
related: ["anthropogenic-climate-change", "ensemble-modelling"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Studies attribute long-term trends, such as global warming, and increasingly individual extreme events, such as heatwaves. Results are usually expressed as how much more likely or intense an event became.
