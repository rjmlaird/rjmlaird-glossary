# Emissivity

**Definition:** Emissivity is the ratio of the thermal radiation a surface emits to what a perfect [[black body]] would emit at the same temperature, between 0 and 1.

**In plain language:** It tells you how efficiently a surface gives off heat as radiation. Satellites that measure surface temperature need to account for emissivity, because different materials radiate differently even at the same temperature.

**Related:** [[black body]], [[albedo]], [[satellite remote sensing]]
