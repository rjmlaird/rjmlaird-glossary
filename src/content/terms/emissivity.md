---
term: "Emissivity"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "Emissivity is the ratio of the thermal radiation a surface emits to what a perfect black body would emit at the same temperature, between 0 and 1."
related: ["black-body", "albedo", "remote-sensing"]
updated: 2026-10-17
---

It tells you how efficiently a surface gives off heat as radiation. Satellites that measure surface temperature need to account for emissivity, because different materials radiate differently even at the same temperature.
