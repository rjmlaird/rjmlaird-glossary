---
term: "Interpolation"
category: earth-observation
aliases: ["spatial interpolation"]
short: "Interpolation estimates values at places with no measurement from values at nearby measured points."
related: ["kriging", "inverse-distance-weighting"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It turns point samples, such as rain gauges, into a continuous surface. Methods differ in their assumptions, so the choice affects the result.
