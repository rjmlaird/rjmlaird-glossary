term: "Earth observation"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "Earth observation is gathering information about the land, oceans and atmosphere, mainly using satellites and other remote sensors."
related: ["remote-sensing"]
updated: 2026-10-20
---

Satellites, aircraft and drones carry instruments that measure how the planet reflects and emits energy. Those measurements are turned into maps and time series, such as land cover, sea temperature, air quality or flood extent. It is the main way to monitor climate and environmental change at global scale.
