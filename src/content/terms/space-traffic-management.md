---
term: "Space traffic management (STM)"
category: space
aliases: ["STM"]
short: "Space traffic management is the set of rules, standards and coordination that allow spacecraft to operate safely and sustainably in orbit."
related: ["space-situational-awareness", "post-mission-disposal"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It covers licensing, data sharing, manoeuvre coordination and end-of-life conduct. There is currently no single global body, and approaches differ between countries.
