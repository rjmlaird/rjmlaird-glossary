short: "Star formation is the process by which dense regions within cold clouds of gas and dust collapse under gravity to form stars."
related: ["spectral-line", "magnitude"]
updated: 2026-10-17
---

Clumps in a cloud pull themselves together, heat up and eventually ignite nuclear fusion. Leftover material can form discs in which planets and smaller bodies such as comets take shape.
