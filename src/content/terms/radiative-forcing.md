---
term: "Radiative forcing"
category: climate-science
aliases: []
short: "Radiative forcing is the change in the Earth's energy balance, in watts per square metre, caused by a driver such as more carbon dioxide, aerosols or a change in solar output."
related: ["greenhouse-effect", "aerosol-forcing", "climate-feedback"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

A positive forcing warms the climate system and a negative one cools it. The IPCC usually measures forcing relative to 1750, and effective radiative forcing also counts rapid adjustments in the atmosphere.
