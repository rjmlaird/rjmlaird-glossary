---
term: "Power-to-X"
category: sustainability
aliases: ["P2X", "power-to-gas"]
short: "Power-to-X means converting electricity into another energy carrier or product, such as hydrogen, ammonia, synthetic fuels or heat."
related: ["green-hydrogen", "electrolysis", "sector-coupling"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

The X is whatever the electricity is turned into. These routes can store renewable energy and decarbonise sectors that are hard to electrify directly, at the cost of conversion losses.
