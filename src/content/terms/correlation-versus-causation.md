---
term: "Correlation versus causation"
category: science-communication
aliases: ["correlation is not causation"]
short: "Correlation versus causation is the distinction between two variables moving together and one actually causing the other."
related: ["selection-bias", "cherry-picking"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

A link can arise from a shared cause, reverse causation or coincidence. Randomised trials and careful causal designs are used to test causes.
