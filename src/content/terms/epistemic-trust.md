---
term: "Epistemic trust"
category: science-communication
aliases: []
short: "Epistemic trust is the willingness to rely on others, such as experts and institutions, as sources of knowledge when you cannot check a claim yourself."
related: ["epistemic-vigilance", "scientific-consensus"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It is needed because nobody can verify everything personally. It can be earned or lost depending on perceived competence, honesty and motives.
