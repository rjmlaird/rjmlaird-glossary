---
term: "Methane slip"
category: climate-science
aliases: []
short: "Methane slip is unburnt methane that escapes in the exhaust of engines and turbines running on natural gas, such as liquefied natural gas ship engines."
related: ["global-warming-potential", "co2-equivalence"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Because methane is a potent greenhouse gas in the short term, slip can cancel much of the carbon dioxide benefit of switching fuel. The amount depends on engine design and operating load.
