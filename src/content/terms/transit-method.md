---
term: "Transit method"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "The transit method detects a planet around another star by measuring the small, periodic dip in the star's brightness when the planet passes in front of it."
related: ["doppler-shift", "magnitude"]
updated: 2026-10-17
---

If a planet crosses our line of sight to its star, it blocks a tiny fraction of the light. The size and timing of the dip indicate the planet's size and orbital period.
