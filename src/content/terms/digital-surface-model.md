---
term: "Digital surface model (DSM)"
category: earth-observation
aliases: ["DSM"]
short: "A digital surface model records the height of the first surface a sensor sees, including buildings, trees and other objects on the ground."
related: ["digital-terrain-model", "digital-elevation-model", "lidar"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It is used for line-of-sight, urban and forestry work. Subtracting a terrain model from it gives the height of objects above ground.
