---
term: "Capacity factor"
category: sustainability
aliases: []
short: "The capacity factor of a power plant is its actual energy output over a period divided by what it would produce running at full rated power throughout."
related: ["intermittency", "levelised-cost-of-energy"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Wind and solar have lower capacity factors than plants that can run continuously, because the resource varies. A low capacity factor is not by itself a measure of poor performance.
