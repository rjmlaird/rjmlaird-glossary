---
term: "Tidal locking"
category: space
aliases: ["synchronous rotation"]
short: "Tidal locking occurs when a body's rotation period matches its orbital period, so that it always shows the same face to the body it orbits."
related: ["habitable-zone"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

The Moon is tidally locked to the Earth. Many planets orbiting close to small, cool stars are expected to be locked.
