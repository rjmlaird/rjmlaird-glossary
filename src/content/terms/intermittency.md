term: "Intermittency"
category: sustainability
aliases: ["variability"]
short: "Intermittency is the variation in output from wind and solar power as weather and time of day change."
related: ["grid-flexibility", "energy-storage", "capacity-factor"]
updated: 2026-10-24

It is partly predictable and averages out across wide areas. Managing it relies on forecasting, storage, interconnection and flexible demand rather than on keeping every plant running continuously.
