short: "A trackable object is an orbiting object large enough for ground sensors to follow reliably, roughly 10 centimetres or more in low Earth orbit."

Smaller fragments are far more numerous but cannot be tracked, so they are modelled statistically. Detection thresholds depend on the radar or telescope.
