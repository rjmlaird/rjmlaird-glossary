---
term: "CO₂ equivalence (CO₂e)"
category: climate-science
aliases: ["CO2e", "carbon dioxide equivalent"]
short: "CO₂ equivalence expresses the warming effect of different greenhouse gases as the mass of carbon dioxide that would have the same effect over a chosen time horizon."
related: ["global-warming-potential", "greenhouse-gas-inventory"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It lets methane, nitrous oxide and other gases be added to carbon dioxide in one figure, using global warming potentials. The result depends on which time horizon and which assessment report's values are used.
