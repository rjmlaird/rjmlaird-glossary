# Black body

**Definition:** A black body is an idealised object that absorbs all radiation falling on it and emits radiation whose spectrum depends only on its temperature.

**In plain language:** No real object is a perfect black body, but stars and planets can often be approximated as one. This lets us estimate temperatures from the colour and intensity of the light they give off.
