---
term: "Astrometry"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "Astrometry is the precise measurement of the positions, distances and motions of stars and other objects."
related: ["photometry", "spectroscopy"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Parallax from the Earth's orbit gives distances to nearby stars. ESA's Gaia mission has measured positions and motions for well over a billion stars.
