# Apogee and perigee

**Definition:** For an object orbiting the Earth, perigee is the point of closest approach and apogee is the point farthest away.

**In plain language:** Orbits are rarely perfect circles. An elliptical orbit swings closer to Earth at perigee, where the object moves fastest, and farther out at apogee, where it moves slowest. Other bodies have their own terms, such as perihelion and aphelion for the Sun.
