# Light pollution

**Definition:** Light pollution is excessive or misdirected artificial light that brightens the night sky and affects people, wildlife and observation.

**In plain language:** Street lighting, signs and buildings scatter light into the sky, hiding faint stars and making astronomy harder. Shielded, well-aimed and appropriately bright lighting reduces the problem and often saves energy.
