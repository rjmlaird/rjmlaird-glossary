---
term: "Demisability"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "Demisability is how readily a spacecraft or its parts break up and burn away during atmospheric re-entry, rather than surviving to reach the ground."
related: ["design-for-demise", "atmospheric-re-entry"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Dense or heat-resistant components such as tanks and some metals are more likely to survive. It is used to assess the casualty risk on the ground.
