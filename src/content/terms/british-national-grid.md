short: "The British National Grid is the projected coordinate system used by Ordnance Survey for Great Britain, giving positions in metres east and north of a false origin."

updated: 2026-10-24

It is identified as EPSG:27700. Grid references use letter pairs for 100 km squares, and the system is accurate for Great Britain but not elsewhere.
