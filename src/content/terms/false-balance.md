---
term: "False balance"
category: science-communication
aliases: ["false equivalence in media"]
short: "False balance is presenting two sides of an issue as equally supported when the weight of evidence is heavily on one side."
related: ["scientific-consensus", "media-framing"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It can give a fringe view disproportionate attention and create a misleading impression of scientific disagreement.
