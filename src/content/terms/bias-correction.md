---
term: "Bias correction"
category: climate-science
aliases: []
short: "Bias correction adjusts climate model output so that its statistics match observations over a reference period before it is used in impact studies."
related: ["downscaling", "ensemble-modelling"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It removes systematic errors, such as a model that runs too cold or too dry, but cannot fix a model that gets the physics wrong. Methods such as quantile mapping are common.
