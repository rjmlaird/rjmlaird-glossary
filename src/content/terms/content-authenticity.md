term: "Content authenticity"
category: science-communication
aliases: []
short: "Content authenticity is being able to verify where a piece of digital content came from and how it was edited."
related: ["provenance", "deepfake", "synthetic-media"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Standards such as C2PA content credentials attach signed provenance information to media. They show origin and edits but do not prove that the content is true.
