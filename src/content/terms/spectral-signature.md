---
term: "Spectral signature"
category: earth-observation
aliases: ["spectral reflectance curve"]
short: "A spectral signature is the pattern of energy a material reflects or emits across wavelengths, which is characteristic of that material."
related: ["multispectral-imaging", "ndvi"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Healthy leaves reflect strongly in the near-infrared and absorb red light, while water absorbs most infrared. Classification and indices such as NDVI exploit these differences.
