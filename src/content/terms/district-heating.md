---
term: "District heating"
category: sustainability
aliases: ["heat network"]
short: "District heating distributes heat from one or more central sources to many buildings through a network of insulated pipes."
related: ["heat-pump", "sector-coupling"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

The heat can come from waste heat, large heat pumps, geothermal or biomass. Networks make most sense in dense areas, where pipe length per customer is short.
