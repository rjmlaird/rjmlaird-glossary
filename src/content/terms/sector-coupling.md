term: "Sector coupling"
category: sustainability
aliases: []
short: "Sector coupling links the power, heat, transport and industry sectors so that electricity, especially from renewables, can be used across all of them."
related: ["electrification", "power-to-x", "grid-flexibility"]
updated: 2026-10-24

It uses technologies such as heat pumps, electric vehicles and hydrogen to add demand that can be flexible and to move surplus renewable power into other uses.
