---
term: "Land surface temperature (LST)"
category: earth-observation
aliases: ["LST"]
short: "Land surface temperature is the radiative temperature of the ground surface, as measured by a thermal sensor."
related: ["emissivity", "atmospheric-correction"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It differs from air temperature, which is measured about two metres above ground. Estimating it from satellite data needs emissivity and atmospheric corrections.
