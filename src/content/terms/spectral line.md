# Spectral line

**Definition:** A spectral line is a narrow feature in a spectrum, appearing as a dark absorption line or bright emission line at a wavelength characteristic of a particular atom or molecule.

**In plain language:** Each substance leaves its own pattern, like a fingerprint. Reading these lines reveals what stars and atmospheres are made of, and shifts in them reveal motion.
