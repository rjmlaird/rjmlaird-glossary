---
term: "Inoculation theory"
category: science-communication
aliases: []
short: "Inoculation theory holds that giving people a weakened form of a persuasive attack, with a refutation, builds resistance to later, stronger attempts."
related: ["prebunking", "debunking"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It was developed by William McGuire in the 1960s using an analogy with vaccines. It underlies prebunking.
