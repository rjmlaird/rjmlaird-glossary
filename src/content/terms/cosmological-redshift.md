term: "Cosmological redshift"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "Cosmological redshift is the stretching of light's wavelength as it travels through expanding space."
related: ["redshift", "hubble-tension"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It is not caused by galaxies moving through space. A redshift of z means the universe has expanded by a factor of 1 + z since the light left.
