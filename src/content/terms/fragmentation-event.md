---
term: "Fragmentation event"
category: space
aliases: ["break-up event"]
short: "A fragmentation event is the break-up of a spacecraft or rocket stage into many pieces, through an explosion, collision or deliberate destruction."
related: ["orbital-debris", "kessler-syndrome"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Well-known examples include the 2007 Chinese anti-satellite test and the 2009 collision of an Iridium satellite with a defunct Russian satellite. Such events are major sources of debris.
