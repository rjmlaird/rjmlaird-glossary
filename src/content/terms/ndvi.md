---
term: "Normalised Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI)"
category: earth-observation
aliases: ["NDVI"]
short: "NDVI is a vegetation index calculated as (near-infrared − red) divided by (near-infrared + red), giving values from −1 to 1."
related: ["evi", "spectral-signature"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Dense, healthy vegetation scores high because it reflects near-infrared and absorbs red. It can saturate in dense canopies, which EVI was designed to reduce.
