term: "Core Web Vitals"

aliases: []
short: "Core Web Vitals are Google's measures of page experience: Largest Contentful Paint for loading, Interaction to Next Paint for responsiveness and Cumulative Layout Shift for visual stability."
related: ["progressive-web-app", "semantic-html"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

Good thresholds are 2.5 seconds or less for LCP, 200 milliseconds or less for INP and 0.1 or less for CLS. Interaction to Next Paint replaced First Input Delay in 2024.
