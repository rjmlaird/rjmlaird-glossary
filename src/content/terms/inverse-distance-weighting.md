---
term: "Inverse distance weighting (IDW)"
category: earth-observation
aliases: ["IDW"]
short: "Inverse distance weighting estimates a value as an average of nearby measurements, weighted so that closer points count for more."
related: ["interpolation", "kriging"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It is quick and easy to apply, but gives no uncertainty estimate and can create bullseye patterns around sample points.
