---
term: "Post-mission disposal"
category: space
aliases: []
short: "Post-mission disposal is removing a spacecraft from useful orbit, or making it safe, at the end of its operational life."
related: ["deorbit", "graveyard-orbit", "orbital-debris"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

In low Earth orbit it usually means lowering the orbit so the spacecraft re-enters. Some regulators and agencies now expect this within about five years, down from the older 25-year guideline.
