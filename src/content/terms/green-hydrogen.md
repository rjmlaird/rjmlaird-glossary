term: "Green hydrogen"
category: sustainability
aliases: []
short: "Green hydrogen is hydrogen made by splitting water with electrolysis powered by renewable or other low-carbon electricity."
related: ["electrolysis", "power-to-x"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It differs from grey hydrogen, made from natural gas with high emissions. Its climate benefit depends on the electricity source and on avoiding leaks of hydrogen itself.
