term: "Viewshed analysis"
category: earth-observation
aliases: ["viewshed"]
short: "Viewshed analysis finds the areas visible from one or more viewpoints, given the terrain and sometimes buildings and trees."
related: ["digital-surface-model", "raster-data"]
updated: 2026-10-24
---

It is used for planning, siting masts and turbines, and assessing visual impact. Results depend on the quality of the surface model and the observer height.
